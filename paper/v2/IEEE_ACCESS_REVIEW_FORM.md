# IEEE Access reviewer instructions (from ieeeaccess.ieee.org reviewer guidelines and the Reviewer Information PDF)

Process: single-anonymized peer review; at least 2 independent reviewers; review due in 7 days.
The Associate Editor (AE) makes a binary decision. Only ONE resubmission is allowed.

## Reviewer questions (answer each)
1. Does the paper contribute to the body of knowledge?
2. Is the paper technically sound?
3. Is the subject matter presented in a comprehensive manner?
4. Are the references provided applicable and sufficient?
5. Are there references that are not appropriate for the topic being discussed?

## Acceptance criteria (rate each: Met / Partly / Not met)
A1. Original research presenting an advance in the field.
A2. Not published or under consideration elsewhere (expanded conference papers OK).
A3. Experiments, statistics and other analyses performed to a high technical standard and described in sufficient detail.
A4. Conclusions presented in an appropriate fashion and supported by the data.
A5. Written in standard English.
A6. Appropriate references to related prior published works.
A7. Within the scope of IEEE Access.

## Evaluation dimensions (Reviewer Information PDF)
- Technical strength: depth of the technical content, analysis, data, benchmarking/validation, technical flaws.
- Contribution: advancement over state of the art, literature review, comparison with state of the art.
- Comprehensive presentation: reporting, logical flow, reproducibility of experiments/simulations.

## Recommendation (choose exactly one)
- Accept (minor edits permitted)
- Reject (updates required before resubmission)
- Reject (resubmission not permitted)

## Required output sections
1. Summary of the paper (3-5 sentences)
2. Answers to Q1-Q5
3. Table of A1-A7 with rating and one-line justification
4. Strengths
5. Major weaknesses (numbered, each with location in the manuscript: section/equation/table)
6. Minor issues
7. Required changes for acceptance (numbered, actionable)
8. Confidential comments to the Associate Editor
9. Recommendation (one of the three options) and confidence (1-5)
